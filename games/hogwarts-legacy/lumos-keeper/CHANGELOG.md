# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-22

### Added

- Lumos stays lit after other spells, rolls and interactions, until Lumos is cast again.
- The wand stays raised while the light is kept.
- Casting Lumos at the kept light puts it out and lowers the wand, from the spell slot or a quick-cast mod such as QuickUtils.
- The light is let go on a mount, and the next cast on foot is kept again.
- Devil's Snares keep recoiling from the kept light after other spells are cast.
